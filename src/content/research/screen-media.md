---
title: "Screen Media Activities"
description: "We study how engagement with screens and digital media—including social media, video games, and streaming—shapes brain development, behavior, and mental health in children and adolescents."
order: 1
---

Adolescents today are immersed in digital environments at an unprecedented scale. Our Screen Media Activities research group investigates how different forms of screen-based media use relate to neurodevelopment, cognitive performance, emotional well-being, and behavioral outcomes. Drawing on large-scale cohort studies and neuroimaging data, we aim to disentangle harmful from benign—or even beneficial—patterns of media engagement and identify the developmental windows during which these effects matter most.
